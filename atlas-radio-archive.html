Atlas 3 Radio

Music is a big passion of mine. It is the ultimate being in the moment medium of art. It is something that is only present in the present, once you hear it it is gone.

Next to that, music can mean different things to people and different times. But is also an universal language.

I personally miss the temporal relation we used to have with music. Performances happened once and were not recorded. Radio is only there when you tune in and you can only access your music if you have your record player or iPod with you.
